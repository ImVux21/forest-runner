⚔️ LEGENDS & MAP OF ELDORIA 🌲

🔮 THE DARK CURSE & SACRED EMERALDS

The ancient realm of Eldoria was long guarded by the sacred Emerald Heart of the Forest. But last night, an ancient Dark Curse awakened, shattering the jewel into emerald shards scattered across the four winds. As midnight deepens, deadly violet mist will rise and claim the forest unless all sacred gems are gathered and the Forest Spirit is awakened!

🗺️ 4 CORE REALMS & 4 EXPANSION OUTLANDS (180M x 180M)

From the central crossroads signpost in the valley, ancient cobblestone trails lead outward in all directions:

🏡 West: Oakvale Village (-60m) A peaceful farming hamlet with stone wells, cozy cottages, and windmills.
🏰 East: Dragon Fortress (+70m) The Black Dragon perches atop a sheer 22m volcanic fortress rock.
🔮 North: Forbidden Forest (+60m) Arcane violet light pillars, domain of the ancient King Alpaking and spectres.
💀 South: Murky Demon Swamp (-60m) A shadowy marsh shrouded in gloom, lair of the Demon, Yeti, and Orcs.
❄️ Northwest: Frostfall Sanctuary (-48m, +44m) An ancient icy lighthouse and radiant sapphire crystals gleaming in the tundra frost.
🌋 Northeast: Molten Crags (+48m, +45m) Fractured basalt columns, lava fissures, and gleaming fire ruby veins.
🍄 Southwest: Elder Shroomwood (-48m, -48m) Colossal 3.2m glowing canopy mushrooms releasing mystical bioluminescent spores.
☀️ Southeast: Sun Temple Ruins (+48m, -48m) An ancient golden marble sanctuary built to honor celestial daylight.

☁️ CELESTIAL SKY ISLANDS (ALTITUDE 14M - 18M)

Explore 3 mystical islands floating amidst the clouds above the kingdom:

• Celestial Sky Temple (y = 16m): Central floating island surrounded by 4 stone monoliths and the rotating Archon Crystal.
• Falcon Crest Roost (y = 14.5m): Northwest aerial observation platform offering a panoramic vista over Oakvale Village.
• Dragon Blood Aerie (y = 18m): High-altitude basalt island hovering above the eastern volcano with eternal sacred flame.

🪂 AERIAL NAVIGATION & SURVIVAL GUIDE

• 🌪️ Updraft Thermal Vents: Step into glowing cyan wind vortexes (at Peak Summit 18, 22 or Crossroads 0, -8) to get catapulted 18m - 22m into the skies!
• 🪂 Falcon Glider: While airborne, HOLD SPACE to deploy your glider and soar effortlessly across chasms.
• ⭕ Aerial Wind Boost Rings: Glide through glowing golden rings suspended between islands for an instant aerial speed boost!
• 🍄 Spring Mushrooms: Step on red spotted mushrooms on the ground to launch yourself onto high plateaus.
